## The workflow

Speak in Arabic or Libyan Arabic, review the proposed reminder, adjust the details and approve it before an alert is scheduled.

The project has native Android and iOS applications. See the [main repository](https://github.com/mohamedkbay/fakkerni) for current releases, platform setup and the full screen tour.
