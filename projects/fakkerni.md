# Fakkerni

**Arabic-first voice reminders for Android and iOS.**

[Source & downloads](https://github.com/mohamedkbay/fakkerni) · [Project gallery](../README.md) · [Full catalog](../PROJECTS.md)

**Availability:** Public source & downloads · native Android and iOS.

## Overview

Speak in Arabic or Libyan Arabic, review the proposed reminder, adjust the details and approve it before an alert is scheduled.
