# مساجدنا · Masajiduna

**An Arabic mobile companion for prayer times and nearby mosques.**

[Visit the website](https://masajiduna.ly) · [Back to selected work](../README.md)

## The idea

Bring prayer times, mosque discovery, daily adhkar and Quran radio into one clear Arabic experience.

مساجدنا يجمع مواقيت الصلاة، البحث عن المساجد القريبة، الأذكار وإذاعة القرآن في تطبيق عربي واحد.

## Development scope

- A Flutter mobile app with local prayer-time calculations and configurable notifications.
- A mosque map with search, distance sorting and directions, using OpenStreetMap / Overpass data.
- Offline adhkar and Quran radio with background audio support.
- A responsive companion website with real app screens and an interactive mosque map.

## Technology

Flutter · Dart · local notification scheduling · OpenStreetMap / Overpass · React · TypeScript

## Availability

The public website presents the app and its screens. Its store buttons currently show **coming soon**. The source repositories remain private.
