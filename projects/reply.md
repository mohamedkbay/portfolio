# REPLY — My Flagship Product

**Product owner & lead developer: [Mohamed Bay](https://github.com/mohamedkbay)**  
**Company:** [Etaar Digital Systems](https://etaar.ly) · **Period:** January 2026–present

REPLY is my main product and ongoing development focus: an Arabic-first workspace that connects a merchant’s products, customer replies and order operations.

**REPLY is my flagship project.** I’m Mohamed Bay, its product owner and lead developer, and I lead its development through Etaar Digital Systems. We are building REPLY to support merchants from the first product inquiry through order review, preparation and delivery follow-up. Our ambition is to make it the first choice for merchants across the Arab world by offering practical value and competitive pricing.

[Visit REPLY](https://raply.ly/) · [Features](https://raply.ly/features) · [Interactive feature tour](https://raply.ly/#tour) · [Plans](https://raply.ly/pricing) · [Start a trial](https://raply.ly/auth/signup) · [Sign in](https://raply.ly/auth/login)

## What I own and build

I own the REPLY product and lead its product direction, interface design, engineering and ongoing development. My work brings together:

- A product catalog with codes, images, prices, availability and product options.
- Product-code answers, editable business replies and keyword automations.
- Customer order collection, merchant review and order confirmation.
- Delivery reports, package labels and a companion workflow for printing.
- Collections and shipping workflows, subject to the plan and carrier setup.
- Arabic/English interfaces, light/dark appearance, responsive web access and mobile application development.

The current work spans TypeScript, Node.js, SQLite, API integrations and an Expo/React Native mobile codebase. Native distribution and individual provider activation have their own requirements.
